# Nexy

Nexy is a fork of VRCX. The fork lives at `valzzu/Nexy` (`origin`); `vrcx-team/VRCX` is `upstream`.

## Never push anything to VRCX

- Never push, open PRs, open issues, comment, or otherwise write to `vrcx-team/VRCX`. No exceptions.
- All pushes and PRs go to `valzzu/Nexy` (`origin`) only.
- Always make PRs unless otherwise told.
- The `upstream` push URL is deliberately disabled; do not re-enable it.
- `gh` may resolve to `vrcx-team/VRCX` by default in this checkout. Always pass `-R valzzu/Nexy` to `gh` commands (PRs, runs, releases, issues, workflow dispatch).
- Do not add CLAUDE as a contributor
